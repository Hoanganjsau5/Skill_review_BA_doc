- **Edition:** Second edition (2023-05-01)

- **Access & Verification Status:** Secondary-source and official catalog verification only. Full ISO/IEC/IEEE copyrighted text is not stored in this workspace. Do not claim verbatim quotations.

## Key Engineering Guidance Extracted
1. **Separation of Verification/Set Quality vs. Validation vs. Traceability:**
   - Distinguishes between ensuring the requirement specification set is well-formed, complete, and internally consistent (`re.requirement-set-quality`), validating that requirements accurately reflect stakeholder needs and mission/business objectives (`re.requirement-validation`), and maintaining structural traceability links across hierarchy layers and verification items (`re.requirement-traceability`).
2. **Traceability Across System Hierarchy Levels:**
   - Emphasizes forward and backward traceability across business/mission objectives, stakeholder requirements, system requirements, subsystem/software elements, and verification cases so that neither unallocated requirements nor unjustified downstream elements remain undetected.
